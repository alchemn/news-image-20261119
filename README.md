Sum up 19 November's world news in one image: a dense newspaper front page for Thursday, 19 November 2026 — 50 stories from seven regions, built on the winning formula of the previous day's contest: 14 illustrated lead panels (one original drawing per story), per-story sources with CLAIM/CONFIRMED labels, a DATE CHECK bar, a UTC timeline, a day-in-figures band, and a natural-proportion world map with numbered pins. Xi, Trump and Putin meet in the first-ever three-way summit in Shenzhen as a tanker is struck in Hormuz, the Crimea bridge shuts again, Anthropic files the biggest IPO of all time at $81.3bn, Voyager 1 passes one light-day from Earth, and Jeffries is set to lead the House — plus 36 more of the day's stories. Original illustration, drawn as a poster in `poster.html` (world map rendered from public-domain Natural Earth data); `main.ts` copies the rendered `entry.png` to the output.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
